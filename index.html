🔥 BC Wildfire Proximity Check

Check whether any active BC wildfire within 50 km of a postal code or a city/province currently has an evacuation order or alert in place.

Include the province or state so the right place is found, e.g. "Kelowna, BC" or "Kelowna, British Columbia"
Fire data: BC Wildfire Service (current fire locations, refreshed ~every 15 min by the province). Evacuation data: Emergency Management and Climate Readiness - Evacuation Orders and Alerts (aggregated from local governments and First Nations; matched to fires by fire number). Location lookup: Zippopotam.us (postal codes) or OpenStreetMap Nominatim (city/province). This tool is for general reference only — fire conditions change quickly and this should never be relied on as your sole source for evacuation or safety decisions. Always check BC Wildfire Service's official dashboard and local emergency alerts.
